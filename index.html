The Disconnect

“The number you received and the number available aren’t the same.”

Here’s a question worth sitting with.

In one scenario, a business sells for $47 million. In another scenario — same business, same industry, similar size, similar financials, same market, same year — that exact company goes to market at $250 million.

What’s the difference?

It’s not luck. It’s not timing. It’s not that one owner negotiated harder.

In the second scenario, someone looked closely. First they alleviated the constraints suppressing the valuation — the quiet problems in operations, financials, market position — and the transferable value rose from $47 million to $100 million.

Then they activated the 26-factor methodology’s value accelerators. The intangibles most advisors don’t even measure: market position, revenue model, culture, data, customer relationships. Things that, once identified and developed, completely change what a sophisticated buyer is willing to pay.

That same company went to market at $250 million.

The $203 million gap — between what’s visible and what’s actually there — is where we work.

The System

Every business runs on three levers. Most businesses have all three misaligned.

01
Marketing

Generates activity without documented proof it drives revenue. Until marketing can prove its through-line to value, buyers treat it as overhead — and price it accordingly.

02
Finance

Tracks the numbers without a clear line to growth strategy. Finance should be the proof layer — the evidence that backs every growth claim. When it isn’t, the gap between what you believe and what a buyer pays widens.

03
Operations

Executes without the capacity to scale — and the founder fills every gap. When operations can’t run without you, buyers see a job, not a business. They pay job multiples for jobs.

When all three are aligned — documented, measurable, and reinforcing each other — that alignment can add a full multiple, sometimes two or more, to your exit valuation.

The ZLV Protocol

Every driver scored. Every gap identified. No guessing.

We run every client through a 26-factor assessment before we do anything else. It scores every dimension a sophisticated buyer should be looking at — and most aren’t. The buyers who do examine these factors pay the highest multiples.

Tier 01
Operational Drivers

The internal fundamentals buyers scrutinize. Financials, technology, operations, human capital, legal, innovation. These determine whether your business runs — and runs well — without you in the room.

Tier 02
Market Drivers

Your external position. Growth trajectory, market dominance, recurring revenue, barriers to entry, customer diversification. This is where most of the hidden value lives — and where most founders are most undervalued.

Tier 03
Value Accelerators

The multipliers that fundamentally shift what a buyer is willing to pay. Impact mission, brand equity, data strategy, culture. In today’s acquisition market, these are increasingly the difference between a 6x multiple and a 12x multiple.

Every factor gets scored. The gaps become your roadmap. The assessment reveals the map. We find the treasure.
