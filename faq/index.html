They're two different numbers. Valuation is a calculation. Transaction value is a negotiation. Your business might be worth $50 million by every reasonable measure. Multiples vary by industry, but the range within any industry is wider than most founders realize. A manufacturing company might sell for 4x EBITDA on the low end and 12x on the high end. A SaaS business might range from 6x to 20x+. The difference isn't the industry — it's the specific combination of value drivers inside the business.

Recurring revenue, customer diversification, management depth, growth trajectory, market position — these push a deal from the bottom of the range to the top. Generic multiples are useful as a baseline. They're not useful as a target. The 26-factor methodology identifies exactly which drivers are suppressing your multiple and which ones could lift it.
