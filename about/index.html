The Origin Story

He learned the most important lesson in M&A at 13.

Steve Little started a lawn care business at 13 years old. Two years later, he sold it for nearly $250,000. Not the equipment. Not the truck. Not the list of customers. The contracts. A buyer offered far more than the obvious assets were worth — because the contracts made the revenue predictable. The business stopped being a lawn care operation and became something far more valuable: a stream of guaranteed income.

That lesson — that the real value of a business is almost never what's visible on the surface — has shaped every engagement Steve has run since.

The Track Record

470+ companies. Average gains of 6x to 10x.

Over the course of his advisory career, Steve and the ZLV team have worked with more than 470 companies. The average valuation gain across those engagements is 6x to 10x compared to unassisted transactions. In dollar terms, that's more than $100 million in additional returns that founders kept — returns they would have left on the table working with a traditional broker. The results aren't uniform. A few examples:

These aren't flukes. They're what happens when the right framework is applied before the business goes to market.
