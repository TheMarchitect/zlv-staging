The Origin Story

He learned the most important lesson in M&A at 13.

Steve Little started a lawn care business at 13 years old. Two years later, he sold it for nearly $250,000.

Not the equipment. Not the truck. Not the list of customers. The contracts.

A buyer offered far more than the obvious assets were worth — because the contracts made the revenue predictable. The business stopped being a lawn care operation and became something far more valuable: a stream of guaranteed income.

That lesson — that the real value of a business is almost never what’s visible on the surface — has shaped every engagement Steve has run since.

Both Sides of the Table

He’s sat in the buyer’s chair. Most advisors haven’t.

Steve has raised over $3 billion in startup and growth funding across industries. He’s founded and scaled six companies to private nine-figure acquisitions. And he’s been on the other side: he once led a buy-side M&A team that acquired and integrated nine companies in twelve months.

That last part matters more than it sounds.

When Steve sits across from a buyer on your behalf, he isn’t guessing what they’re thinking. He’s been the one doing the thinking. He knows which questions get asked in diligence. He knows which red flags trigger discounts. He knows which value drivers get a buyer to move faster and pay more — because he’s the one who acted on them.

That’s a different kind of advisor.

The Track Record

470+ companies. Average gains of 6x to 10x.

Over the course of his advisory career, Steve and the ZLV team have worked with more than 470 companies. The average valuation gain across those engagements is 6x to 10x compared to unassisted transactions. In dollar terms, that’s more than $100 million in additional returns that founders kept — returns they would have left on the table working with a traditional broker or going to market unprepared.

A technology services company projected to sell for $2 million was restructured as a SaaS platform. It sold for $120 million. A regional trucking company was repositioned as an Energy Logistics Supplier and closed at 7X its original multiple, no earn-out required. An eCommerce retailer valued at under 1X revenue had 16 internal constraints removed and became a $1.6 billion NASDAQ IPO target at 29X.

These aren’t flukes. They’re what happens when the right framework is applied before the business goes to market.

The Methodology

He didn’t just develop a system. He wrote the book on it.

Steve is the author of The M&A Multiplier Playbook, a proprietary framework for identifying and removing the factors that suppress business valuations. The 26-factor methodology at the heart of ZLV’s work — organized into three tiers of Operational Drivers, Market Drivers, and Value Accelerators — was developed from Steve’s direct experience on both sides of hundreds of transactions.

He has mentored more than 100 business owners and produced dozens of presentations, webinars, and masterclasses on the methodology. He speaks and teaches because he believes most founders are operating with an incomplete picture of what their businesses are actually worth — and that the gap between what they believe and what’s possible is almost always larger than they expect.

What It’s Like to Work With Him

He’s not a broker. He’s a builder.

Steve doesn’t take listings and shop them to buyers. He doesn’t work on commission from a 3-month sprint to close. He doesn’t optimize for volume.

He works with a small number of founder-owners at a time, each engagement running 6 to 12 months. The process is collaborative and diagnostic — starting with the 26-factor assessment, building a clear picture of where the value is and where it’s being discounted, and then executing the preparation strategy before the company ever goes to market.

By the time a ZLV client is in front of buyers, they’re not negotiating from weakness. They’re negotiating with leverage. That’s by design.
