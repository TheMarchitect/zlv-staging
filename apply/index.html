What This Is

Here's what happens in a ZLV Strategy Session.

You come in with your 26-factor assessment results. A ZLV advisor walks through them with you. That means looking at exactly which factors are suppressing your current multiple and which ones are working in your favor. It means identifying the two or three highest-leverage changes your business could make before going to market. It means an honest picture of what preparation would realistically look like — the timeline, the work involved, the potential upside. No vague promises. No inflated projections. No pressure to sign anything.

Common Questions

A few questions we hear before founders apply.

Is my business big enough?

If you're at $15M+ in revenue and thinking seriously about an exit, yes. The session isn't about the size. It's about whether there's meaningful upside.

Is my business old enough?

Age matters less than trajectory. A 4-year-old company on a strong growth curve is often better positioned than a 20-year-old business that's plateaued.

Is my business successful enough?

The session is often most valuable when things are messy. A business with obvious gaps has obvious upside. The gaps are where the opportunity lives.

Am I ready?

You don't need to have everything figured out. You just need your assessment results and a serious interest in what comes next. If you're not sure you qualify, submit the application. Let us make that determination.
